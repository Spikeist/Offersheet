## Market-blended projections

CLAUDE.md §6 step 8 (planner reply to Checkpoint 4, change 1): every QB, RB, WR, TE with a real FantasyCalc value in this season's list for the league's format has his points per game pulled part of the way toward what our base rates give healthy players valued like him: b′ = (1 − w)·b + w·R_pos(v), w = 0.50 (config.marketBlend.weight 0.5), R_pos the isotonic curve of base rates on value over his position's healthy valued players (each read off the curve fitted without him, except at the two ends of the value range, who read it with their own point in it). Then the factor f = b′ / b is clamped to [0.75, 1.35] (config.marketBlend.factorClamp; Phase 4c, planner reply to Checkpoint 4b, change 4: "Extreme factors are where data glitches and market hype concentrate"), so a clamped player's base rate is b × f, not b′. His weekly points and ROS points scale by f, so byes, availability and week shape stay the model's, and every team number (lineups, needs, chips, V*, the odds) reads the blended points.
