## IR returners

CLAUDE.md §6–§7 (planner reply to Checkpoint 4, change 5): an IR player with a model rejoins his team's lineups from his expected return week R, the first remaining week his chance of being back reaches 50% (availability.expectedReturnWeek; his own availability keeps him out before it).
