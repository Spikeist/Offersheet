## Depth: a bench player's value to his team, EV lineups vs scenario lineups

MV_lose = the team's rest-of-season lineup points with him, minus without him (after it adds the best free agent). EV lineups only count him when he starts in the average case; scenario lineups also count the weeks he fills in for an injured or bye-week starter.

Bench players here are those whose lineup cost under EV lineups is at most 2 lineup points: the average-case lineup almost never starts them. Lineup cost is measured from the team after its best free-agent move (below), so it is never negative.

Depth value = his team's rest-of-season lineup points with him minus without him, with nobody added in his place: the pure effect of scenario lineups. Lineup cost (§7 MV_lose) also lets the team add the best free agent, so in a deep 10-team waiver pool it is smaller. Both are lineup points with playoff weeks counted 1.25×.
