# Draft data: the data check for Phase 8 (the draft assistant)

Checkpoint 7h, section 6 (planner reply to Checkpoint 7g): "Find free sources whose terms allow our use, for: average draft position, by scoring format and league size; preseason season-long projections (Sleeper's and ESPN's season projections); live draft picks: Sleeper's draft endpoints, ESPN's draft data and Yahoo's draft results. For each source, report its terms, what it covers, and a sample."

Probed live on 2026-10-19 and 2026-10-20 UTC (NFL week 6; the last run, on 2026-10-20, after the review of Checkpoint 7h: MFL's league size and scoring each changed alone, and its QBs against Sleeper's 1QB and superflex ADP) with `npm run probe:draft -- --live` (`scripts/probe-draft.ts`): one request at a time per host, cached (ADP lists a day, projections 6 hours; a run sends about 60 requests, cached ones included). Evidence for every check (URL template, HTTP status, JSON path, sample): `reports/probe-output/draft.md` and `draft.json`; each source's first rows: `reports/probe-output/draft.samples.md`. Terms were read on 2026-10-20 from the pages linked below and are quoted word for word. Placeholders only: no league id, draft id, user id, team, owner or league name.

Legend: ✅ confirmed with data · ⚠️ partial, derived, small sample, or needs a decision · ❌ missing · ➖ not applicable · ⏸ not testable now (the reason is given).

**The timing caveat.** Drafts run from late July to early September. In October the draft-season numbers are mostly gone or frozen, so the formats, league sizes and spreads were also checked on last season's (2025) final lists where a source keeps them, and the live-pick paths on completed drafts. What only a running draft can show (picks appearing while a draft is live) is ⏸ until Parker runs a Sleeper mock draft (`npm run probe:draft -- --live --sleeper-draft <its draft id>`).

## Terms, quoted

- **Sleeper** (https://docs.sleeper.com): "The Sleeper API is a read-only HTTP API that is free to use for non-commercial purposes." "For commercial use of the Sleeper API, please reach out to us directly to discuss licensing." "Be mindful of the frequency of calls. A general rule is to stay under 1000 API calls per minute, otherwise, you risk being IP-blocked." The draft endpoints are documented (`/v1/league/<league_id>/drafts`: "Drafts are sorted by most recent to earliest. Most leagues should only have one draft."; `/v1/draft/<draft_id>`, `/picks`, `/traded_picks`, `/v1/user/<user_id>/drafts/<sport>/<season>`). ⚠️ The season projections and their `adp_*` fields are not in the docs (the same undocumented endpoint the app already reads, CLAUDE.md §2.3).
- **ESPN** (Disney's terms of use, https://disneytermsofuse.com/english/, which name ESPN among "Disney Products"): users may not "access, monitor, copy or extract the Disney Products using a robot, spider, script, or other automated means" (2.B.x), nor "use the Disney Products for any commercial or business-related use or build a business utilizing the Disney Products" (2.B.viii). ⚠️ The same standing risk as the app's ESPN import (§19: "the API is unofficial and can change or break at any time"); a draft feature adds the league-independent pool's ADP and projections to what Offer Sheet reads from ESPN.
- **Fantasy Football Calculator** (https://help.fantasyfootballcalculator.com/article/42-adp-rest-api): "Use of the ADP REST API is free for personal and commercial use. Fantasy Football Calculator requests that you provide attribution back to us in the form of a link or mention of some kind." "Please do not call this API too frequently. The data only updates once per day." The probe checks those four sentences are still on the page (DOCS.ffc ✅).
- **MFL** (https://api.myfantasyleague.com/2026/api_info): forbidden: "Harvesting league and/or user data" and "Accessing the API via Javascript from web pages outside the myfantasyleague.com domain." Practices: "Wait one second between making requests and you should be ok." "If a request fails, don't retry." "Your application should request that info no more than once a day and keep it for that long." The `adp` export: "ADP results, including when the result were last updated, how many drafts the player was selected in, the average pick, minimum pick and maximum pick." PERIOD "returns draft data for just drafts that started after the specified period. Valid values are ALL, RECENT, DRAFT, JUNE, JULY, AUG1, AUG15, START, MID, PLAYOFF. This option is not valid for previous seasons." So MFL's ADP is read on the server, once a day (the probe checks the docs: DOCS.mfl ✅).
- **Yahoo** (https://sports.yahoo.com/developer/docs/): `/fantasy/v2/league/{league_key}/draftresults`: "Draft results for all teams in the league."; `/fantasy/v2/team/{team_key}/draftresults`: "List of players drafted by the team." Each result has `pick`, `round`, `team_key`, `player_key` and `cost`; a player's `draft_analysis` has `average_pick`, `average_round`, `percent_drafted` and `average_cost` (Yahoo's own ADP). The API needs an approved app (§2.4) and its terms (https://legal.yahoo.com/us/en/yahoo/terms/product-atos/fantasysportsapi/index.html; the page answered 404 to our fetch on 2026-10-20) require the "Fantasy data provided by Yahoo Fantasy" attribution (§19). Untested: the app isn't approved.
- **FantasyPros**: its terms (https://www.fantasypros.com/about/legal/, section 6): "Except for a single copy made for personal use only, you may not copy, reproduce, modify, republish, upload, post, transmit, or distribute any documents or information from this site in any form or by any means without prior written permission from us or the specific content provider, …". Its API (https://www.fantasypros.com/api-data/): the free key is "Non-production use" with "All endpoints, sample data"; production keys come with its paid HOF membership for "Personal and non-commercial apps"; the commercial plan carries the "Commercial license & redistribution rights". Not a source for a public app: no request sent.
- **Underdog** (https://legal.underdogsports.com, section 6, User Conduct): users may not "scrape, access, monitor, index, frame, link, or copy any content or information on the Services by accessing the Services in an automated way, using any robot, spider, scraper, web crawler, or using any method of access other than manually accessing the publicly-available portions of the Services through a browser or accessing the Services through any approved mobile application, application programming interface, or client application". No public API: no request sent.
- **DynastyProcess** (https://github.com/dynastyprocess/data): GPL-3.0; files "updated via Github Actions on a weekly basis". `values.csv` (dynasty and redraft values with expert ranks) and `db_fpecr_latest.csv` (FantasyPros' expert consensus rankings, re-published) hold no ADP column. ⚠️ Not a source for us: expert ranks aren't ADP, and the rank file is FantasyPros' content, whose terms are above.

## Findings

**ADP**
- **Sleeper** (`/projections/nfl/{season}`, the season rows' `stats.adp_*`; ADP.sleeper.*): 12 keys; real values (below 999, Sleeper's "none") on adp_ppr 2,498 players, adp_half_ppr 2,130, adp_2qb 1,633, adp_dynasty_ppr 1,502, adp_dynasty_2qb 1,486, adp_idp_1qb 1,036, adp_dynasty_half_ppr 757, adp_idp 747, adp_dynasty_std 482, adp_std 310; adp_dynasty and adp_rookie read 999 on every row. ⚠️ The formats are read from the key names (2qb = superflex). One list per format, no league size, no spread. In season the ADP still moves (16 of 40 QBs' adp_ppr the same as on 2026-09-30) while the projection doesn't (40 of 40).
- **ESPN** (`leaguedefaults/3`, `kona_player_info`; ADP.espn.*): one `ownership.averageDraftPosition` per player (500 of 500 in the pool, 1.8 to 170) with `auctionValueAverage`; no format or size variant (⚠️ none among the fields). ESPN's own pre-draft ranks come by format (`draftRanksByRankType`: STANDARD, PPR, SUPERFLEX, ELIMINATION): rankings, not ADP. Last season's ADP isn't kept: all 500 players read 170. 2027 answers 404 (`GENERAL_NOT_FOUND`) until ESPN opens the season.
- **Fantasy Football Calculator** (ADP.ffc.*): each list is a recent window of drafts (`meta.start_date`–`end_date`). In October 2026: PPR 29 players from 109 drafts (Sept 18–25), standard 118, half-PPR 54, 2QB 131, dynasty and rookie 0. Last season's final lists (Aug 25 – Sept 1, 2025): PPR 249 players from 8,470 drafts, standard 221 / 2,017, half-PPR 156 / 718, 2QB 215 / 3,317, dynasty 85 / 125, rookie 34 / 260. Every player carries `stdev`, `high`, `low` and `times_drafted` (Ja'Marr Chase 1.5 ± 0.8, picks 1–5, 1,800 times; at pick 50 DeVonta Smith 48.3 ± 5.3, 34–68): what "the chance he lasts to my pick" needs. ⚠️ The `teams` parameter doesn't change the list: 8, 10, 12 and 14 teams gave the same 8,470 drafts in the same order (`meta.teams` 12) in 2025, and the same 109 drafts in October 2026. `position` filters (✅). No id the crosswalk knows: names, position and team (✅ 28/28 this season; ⚠️ 170/200 on last season's list, whose misses are players on another team now).
- **MFL** (ADP.mfl.*): MFL's default window holds next to nothing in October (0 players from 1 draft), so `PERIOD=ALL`: every draft since June 370 players / 6,733 drafts; 12-team PPR redraft real drafts 451 / 716; 10-team non-PPR 284 / 195; mock drafts 284 / 34. Last season: 12-team PPR redraft 457 / 844, 10-team PPR redraft 354 / 504, 10-team non-PPR 276 / 206. The league size alone splits the list (12 against 10 teams, both PPR: Spearman 0.985 over the first 150, median gap 3.2 picks), and so does the scoring alone (10 teams, PPR against non-PPR: 0.970, 3.6 picks): a list per league size and format. ⚠️ IS_KEEPER K (keeper) and R (rookie) are documented, not checked (only N was asked for). ⚠️ No filter by QB count (api_info's adp export: PERIOD, FCOUNT, IS_PPR, IS_KEEPER, IS_MOCK, CUTOFF, DETAILS), so a list mixes 1QB and superflex leagues: of the first 12 QBs on the 12-team PPR redraft list, 9 (2026) and 12 (2025) sit closer to Sleeper's superflex ADP than to its 1QB ADP (Josh Allen 4.0 in 2026, against Sleeper's 1QB 21.3 and superflex 3.7; ADP.mfl.qb). Its QBs go far earlier than in a 1QB league. Each player: `averagePick`, `minPick`, `maxPick`, `draftsSelectedIn`, `draftSelPct`, `rank`; plus average auction values (`aav`, 358 players, for auction drafts later). MFL ids map through the crosswalk (189/189 and 191/191), but for MFL's team units (ids 0501–0532 and 0716, ⚠️ read as team defenses), which need MFL's players export for their NFL team.
- **How they agree** (ADP.agreement, ⚠️ derived; the players in both lists' first 150, same season only): Sleeper adp_ppr against FFC PPR 0.957 (2025, 121 players, median gap 6.4 picks), against MFL 12-team PPR 0.911 (2025) and 0.917 (2026); ESPN's one list against Sleeper adp_ppr 0.883 (median gap 18.4 picks) and MFL 0.876: ESPN's mixes every format, and MFL's mixes 1QB and superflex leagues (its QBs go early: above).

**Preseason season projections**
- **Sleeper** (PROJ.sleeper.*): 644 rows with projected stats (55 stat keys: QB 78, RB 143, WR 219, TE 124, K 45, DEF 32), from `company` rotowire, scoreable with any league's rules (CLAUDE.md §5), with Sleeper's own pts_ppr, pts_half_ppr and pts_std. In season the season row is neither the sum of the weekly rows nor rest of season (QBs: season ÷ weeks 1–18 0.94, ÷ weeks 6–18 1.31) and hasn't moved since 2026-09-30, with gp 18: ⚠️ read as the preseason projection, kept all season. Last season's rows are still served (last rewritten 2026-01-04: ⚠️ whether preseason or a late update can't be told). 2027: 3,274 rows, none with a projection or ADP yet.
- **ESPN** (PROJ.espn.season): `stats[statSourceId 1, statSplitTypeId 0]` on 391 of the 500 pool players, ESPN stat ids (scoreable through `src/adapters/espn/scoringMap.ts`) and an `appliedTotal` in ESPN's default scoring. In season it's rest of season (Phase 0), so before week 1 it's the full season (⚠️ inferred: no preseason copy seen). Last season's lines are still served; they aren't the sum of the weekly lines served now.

**Live draft picks**
- **Sleeper** (SETUP.sleeper.draft, LIVE.sleeper.*): `GET /v1/draft/{draftId}` gives type (snake, linear, auction: ⚠️ from the docs and one linear draft), status, teams, rounds, `reversal_round`, `pick_timer`, the roster slots, `draft_order` (user → slot; null until set) and `slot_to_roster_id`; `/picks` gives every pick in order (`pick_no`, `round`, `draft_slot`, `roster_id`, `picked_by`, `player_id`, `is_keeper`, and the player's name, position and team in `metadata`, so no players DB is needed); `/traded_picks` which slot belongs to whom. Both answer `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`, so each user's browser can poll a live draft itself. ⏸ Picks during a live draft, and whether `/v1/user/{user_id}/drafts/nfl/{season}` lists mock drafts (it needs Parker's own user id).
- **ESPN** (SETUP.espn.draft, LIVE.espn.*): `settings.draftSettings` gives type (SNAKE here), `pickOrder` (team ids in draft order), date, `timePerSelection`, `keeperCount`, `auctionBudget`; `draftDetail` gives `drafted`, `inProgress` and 150 picks (`overallPickNumber`, `roundId`, `roundPickNumber`, `teamId`, `playerId`, `keeper`, `bidAmount`, …; `memberId` is a SWID: a secret, §0.5). Team units have negative ids (−16000 − team a D/ST; −15000 − team a team QB, ⚠️ by its lineup slot). The view answers the app's own origin (CORS). ⏸ Whether picks appear while `inProgress` is true: ESPN's draft room has its own live connection.
- **Yahoo**: docs only (above), ⏸ until approval.

## What Phase 8 would use

- **ADP:** Sleeper's `adp_*` for the format (it covers every format a league can have, keyed to the app's own ids), with ⚠️ Fantasy Football Calculator's spread for the chance a player lasts (its PPR, standard, half-PPR and 2QB lists; attribution owed) and MFL's per-size lists (FCOUNT 8–16; 10 against 12 teams checked) to check or adjust for league size, ⚠️ its QBs left out of that (its lists mix superflex leagues in: Sleeper's own adp_2qb serves superflex leagues). ESPN's single ADP for an ESPN league is optional (unofficial, every format mixed). FantasyPros, Underdog and DynastyProcess's rank files aren't usable.
- **Projections:** Sleeper's season projections scored with the league's rules (the app's own scoring engine), averaged with ESPN's for ESPN leagues by the agreement rule the in-season model uses (Section 6), ⚠️ a Phase 8 decision.
- **Live picks:** Sleeper first (its picks endpoint, polled from the user's browser), then ESPN's `mDraftDetail` once a live draft has been watched, then Yahoo after approval; manual entry everywhere.
- **Before the draft season:** next season's numbers aren't out in October (Sleeper's 2027 rows hold none; ESPN's 2027 is 404): ⚠️ watch from spring, when the planned mock-draft testing needs them. Nothing keeps preseason numbers yet: an archive of each source's August lists (like the projection snapshots, §2.3) would make a backtest of the draft strategies possible (⚠️ the lists served now are last season's latest copies, not what drafters saw in August).
