## 7. Consistency check: no B from free-agent pickups

**By construction.** B compares your team before and after the trade, and both sides of that comparison are free-agent-optimized rosters (V*, §7): the baseline is every team at V*(T) (evaluate.ts buildTradeContext: each team's weekly scores from its V*(T) lineups, one common-random-numbers matrix), and every trade's "after" is legality's roster run through the same optimizer from scratch, for you and for the partner (evaluateExact: `optimizer.optimize(post-legality roster)`), simulated on the same seasons. With availability scenarios, V* picks its moves by their gain in the same scenario lineups B is measured in, on both sides, so a pickup you could make anyway (a depth pickup included) is made in both rosters, moves both odds alike and cancels in ΔPO and ΔCH; a trade gets credit only for what the pickups can't do. (An open roster spot the trade creates is worth what the best free agent adds there, as §7 says.) **N10** (⚠️ ours, reported to the planner) closes the same gap in the market term: B's market term counts each player you give at no less than the waiver credit W, because a player worth less than the best free agent is replaceable and V*(you) already replaces him, so dumping him isn't a value gain either (the card's values and g_mkt stay the market's).

**The tests.** (1) `src/engine/evaluate.test.ts`, "the simulation behind B (M4)" › "Checkpoint 4: a trade whose whole effect a free-agent pickup reproduces gets no B from it (ΔPO = ΔCH = 0 exactly)": my weak WR2 and a free-agent WR that V*(me) picks up (dropping my bench WR); a partner's bench WR identical to that free agent in every way but his id. Trading my bench WR for him does exactly what the pickup does; it asserts V*(me) makes that one pickup, that after the trade V* picks up nobody, that my weekly score means are equal before and after, and that ΔPO, ΔCH, ΔEW and B_win are exactly 0 at 5,000 seasons. Next to it, "a no-op (my V* and the partner's unchanged) moves no odds" asserts ΔPO = ΔCH = ΔEW = 0 exactly at both simulation counts. (2) In scenario lineups: `src/engine/evaluate.consistency.test.ts`, "Checkpoint 4 consistency in scenario lineups" › "a depth pickup a trade reproduces gets no B: ΔPO = ΔCH = ΔEW = 0 exactly": a free-agent TE who never starts in the average-case lineup but plays when an RB is hurt; V*(me) picks him up by his scenario gain (under 3 in expected-value lineups, over it in scenario ones), and trading the player that pickup drops for a partner's identical TE gives B_win 0 exactly at 5,000 seasons. (3) N10: `src/engine/evaluate.addendum.test.ts`, "B's market term with my given players at max(v, W) (N10)" › "a player V* drops anyway, worth less than W, given for nothing: B = 0 at Low, Medium and High" (before the addendum the same trade scored B > 0 from the open spot's credit).
